The process

It runs on what you already have.

No new platform to learn. No rip-and-replace. The agents sit on top of SAP, M365, your WMS, and QuickBooks — reading what's there, writing back what you approve.

1

Your existing stack

SAP, M365, WMS, QBO, Verkada, custom — whatever you run. We connect read-only by default, with credentials encrypted and revocable at any time.

2

Continuous agent layer

Agents watch overnight — inbox, tickets, pallet flow, AR aging, temperature events, customer signals — learning your operating rhythm.

3

Cross-system threading

A customer email referencing an SAP order with a WMS exception becomes one follow-up, not three. The agent stitches the story before you wake up.

4

Your morning, done

Three to five items that need a decision, drafted actions for each, and alerts on your channel of choice — email, SMS, Teams, push.

🔌

What actually gets connected

OAuth or service accounts on the systems you already run. No new hardware on most engagements. No data leaves your tenant unless you say so. White-glove install and tuning by the Tec-Tel team — the same people who run the integration when the AI isn't involved.
